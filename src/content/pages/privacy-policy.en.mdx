## Introduction

Metro Typing (metrotyping.org) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the site, why it is used, which third parties may receive it, and how you can control cookies and advertising choices.

You can use the main site without creating an account. This policy applies to Metro Typing pages and does not replace the privacy policy of the original game embedded from metrotyping.kr.

## Information We May Process

Depending on the features active on a page, the site or its service providers may process:

1. **Usage information** — pages viewed, visit time, referring page, interactions, and approximate region.
2. **Device and browser information** — browser type, operating system, screen size, language, and technical identifiers.
3. **Network and security information** — IP address, request logs, error data, and signals used to detect fraud, abuse, or invalid traffic.
4. **Preferences** — language, theme, cookie choices, and other settings stored in cookies or local storage.
5. **Advertising and analytics identifiers** — cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies used by Google or other approved providers when those services are active.

We do not sell personal information.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, tags, software development identifiers, and web beacons. Metro Typing and its providers may use them for the following categories:

- **Strictly necessary and security** — keep the site working, remember essential settings, prevent abuse, and protect the service.
- **Preferences** — remember language, appearance, and consent choices.
- **Analytics** — understand traffic, page performance, errors, and how visitors use the site.
- **Advertising** — select and deliver ads, limit how often an ad appears, measure performance, report results, detect invalid traffic, and, when permitted, personalize advertising.

Cookie duration varies. Some cookies end when the browser session closes; others remain for a defined period so preferences, measurement, fraud prevention, or advertising controls continue to work.

## Third-Party Advertising, Cookies, and Web Beacons

Metro Typing may use Google AdSense and related Google advertising services. **Third-party advertisers and advertising technology providers, including Google, may place or read cookies in your browser, use web beacons, process IP addresses, or use device and browser identifiers when ads are requested or displayed on this site.**

These technologies may be used to:

- serve contextual or personalized ads where permitted;
- measure ad delivery, clicks, conversions, and performance;
- limit repeated ad exposure;
- prevent fraud, invalid traffic, and abuse;
- create aggregated reporting for publishers and advertisers.

Google may use information from this site together with information from other sites or apps that use Google services, subject to Google's policies and the choices available to you. Learn more at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google advertising technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can manage ad personalization at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## Analytics

The site may use Google Analytics or privacy-focused analytics services. Analytics providers may process page views, device information, approximate location, referral data, and interaction events to help us understand site performance. Analytics data is used to improve navigation, content, reliability, and user experience rather than to identify visitors by name.

## Consent and Cookie Choices

Where required by law, Metro Typing asks for consent before using cookies or identifiers for personalized advertising or analytics. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the site is configured to use Google Privacy & Messaging, a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework, after the applicable message is published in AdSense.

The consent message may allow you to consent, refuse, or manage purposes and vendors. Your choice is stored so it can be respected on later visits. You can change or withdraw an applicable choice using the **Privacy & cookie settings** control in the site footer. If the consent interface is unavailable, the control opens this policy so you can review browser and provider controls.

You can also:

- block or delete cookies in your browser settings;
- use Google Ad Settings to control ad personalization;
- use device-level privacy or tracking controls where available.

Blocking some cookies may affect language, appearance, analytics, advertising, or other site features. Non-personalized advertising may still require cookies or local storage for fraud prevention, frequency control, and measurement where legally permitted.

## Legal Bases for Applicable Regions

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on consent, legitimate interests in operating and securing the site, performance of a requested service, or compliance with legal obligations. Advertising personalization and non-essential analytics rely on consent where required.

## Embedded Game and External Links

The playable area on the homepage loads the original game from metrotyping.kr in an iframe. The embedded service may receive technical request data and may use its own cookies, storage, or analytics under its own privacy policy. Metro Typing does not control the original service's data practices.

External links, including the Chrome Web Store, Google policy pages, and other game sites, are governed by their own policies.

## Data Sharing and Retention

Information may be shared with hosting, security, analytics, advertising, and technical service providers only as needed to operate the site, comply with law, protect users, or deliver the selected service. Providers may process data in other countries subject to their contractual and legal safeguards.

We retain data only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including security, reporting, troubleshooting, legal compliance, and fraud prevention. Retention periods may also be set by third-party providers under their own policies.

## Security and Children

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the site and its logs. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Metro Typing is a general-audience site and is not directed to children for the purpose of collecting personal information. We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when site features, providers, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of the page shows the latest revision.
